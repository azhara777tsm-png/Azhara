Admissions

Applications for the 2027 intake are open until 30 June 2027. We review every application individually and reply within 14 days.

How to apply

  1. Create your account

    Fill in the registration form with your details and chosen program.

  2. Send your documents

    Upload your school certificate and a short motivation letter.

  3. Take the entrance test

    A 90-minute test in math and logic, online or on campus.

  4. Get your decision

    We email you an offer and enrolment instructions.

Common questions

No. Our first year covers the fundamentals for every program.

Yes. Part-time and evening study modes are available for all programs.
